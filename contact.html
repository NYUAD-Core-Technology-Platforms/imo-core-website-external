Visiting the core

The MPM Core is located on the NYU Abu Dhabi campus on Saadiyat Island. Visitors from outside the university should contact us in advance so that campus access can be arranged. Sample drop-off times and locations are confirmed when a project is scheduled.

Sample shipping

Please do not ship samples without prior agreement. Shipping instructions, including address, packaging and temperature requirements, are provided after consultation and are summarised in the sample-preparation guidelines under Resources.
