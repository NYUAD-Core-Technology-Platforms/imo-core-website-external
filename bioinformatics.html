Bioinformatics & Data Analysis

Data generation is only half of the work. Our bioinformatics team turns raw instrument output into processed, statistically sound and interpretable results, and helps you design studies that will answer your question.

Proteomics data analysis

Peptide and protein identification, label-free and labelled quantification, PTM site localisation, differential expression and quality reports.

Metabolomics data processing

Peak picking, alignment, annotation against spectral libraries, normalisation and quantification for targeted and untargeted datasets.

Genomics / bioinformatics pipelines

Read QC, alignment, variant calling, transcript quantification, differential expression and single-cell analysis using reproducible pipelines.

Statistical analysis

Univariate and multivariate statistics, power analysis, batch-effect assessment and correction, and clear reporting of results.

Multi-omics integration

Integration of proteomics, metabolomics and genomics data, pathway and network analysis, and biological interpretation.

Data visualization

Publication-quality figures, interactive reports and dashboards that make complex datasets easy to explore and communicate.

Experimental design consultation

The best time to involve bioinformatics is before samples are collected. We help you define hypotheses, choose controls and replicates, estimate the number of samples needed, plan for batch effects and select the acquisition strategy that will give analysable data.

What you receive

  • Processed data in standard formats together with the raw instrument files
  • A methods description suitable for publications and grant reports
  • Quality-control summaries and statistical results
  • Figures and, where appropriate, interactive reports
  • A results discussion with the analyst who processed your data

Training

Prefer to analyse your own data? Our courses on proteomics, metabolomics and genomics data analysis and on bioinformatics for multi-omics teach the tools and workflows we use.
