Acknowledgement and citation guidelines

Acknowledging the core in publications, posters and presentations is essential: it documents the facility's contribution and supports continued investment in instruments, staff and training. Please use the following statement, or an equivalent, whenever IMO Core instruments, data or expertise contributed to your work:

"This work was supported by the Integrated Multi-Omics (IMO) Core, part of the Core Technology Platforms at New York University Abu Dhabi."

Where core staff made a substantial intellectual contribution, for example to experimental design, method development or data analysis and interpretation, co-authorship should be discussed at the start of the project. Please also send us a copy of any publication that used the core.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use the IMO Core?

Researchers at NYU Abu Dhabi and external academic, clinical and industry collaborators. Contact us to discuss access and pricing for your group.

How do I start a project?

Complete the project consultation form. We will arrange a meeting to discuss your research question and recommend a platform and workflow.

I don't know whether I need proteomics or metabolomics. Can you help?

Yes. That is exactly what the consultation is for. Describe your question and samples and we will advise on the most suitable approach.

Can I operate the instruments myself?

Some instruments are available to trained users after completing the relevant hands-on course and an assessment. Others are operated by core staff only.

How will I receive my data?

Raw and processed data are delivered through secure institutional storage, together with analysis reports and a methods description.

Do you offer training?

Yes. See the Training & Workshops page for the course catalogue and upcoming dates.
