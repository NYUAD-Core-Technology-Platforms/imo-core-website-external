Submit a Project

Not sure which technology you need? Tell us about your research question and we will recommend the appropriate platform and workflow.

Research questionWhat do you want to find out?
Sample typeCells, tissue, plasma, bacteria, plants, synthetic compounds...
Number of samplesGroups, replicates and time points.

Expected outcomeWhat result would answer your question?

Project consultation request

Submitting opens a pre-filled email to the IMO Core in your mail client. We aim to respond within a few working days to arrange a consultation.

Your email client should now open with the request. If it did not, please email us directly at the core.

What happens next

  1. We review your request and may ask a few follow-up questions.
  2. We schedule a consultation to discuss design, platform and workflow.
  3. You receive a recommended approach, sample-preparation guidance and a cost estimate.
  4. Samples are submitted, data acquired, analysed and discussed with you.

Before you submit samples

Please read the sample-preparation guidelines and submission requirements in Resources. Good samples are the biggest determinant of good data.
