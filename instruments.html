Instruments

The platforms behind our services. If you are not sure which instrument you need, start from the applications guide or discuss your project with us.

SCIEX QTRAP 7500+

Targeted quantitative LC-MS/MS

Hybrid triple-quadrupole / linear ion-trap mass spectrometer for highly sensitive, quantitative LC-MS/MS of small molecules and peptides.

Applications: Metabolomics, biomarker quantification, small molecules, targeted proteomics, lipidomics

View capabilities Discuss a project

timsTOF

High-resolution LC-MS/MS with trapped ion mobility

Trapped-ion-mobility quadrupole time-of-flight mass spectrometer for high-speed, high-sensitivity discovery proteomics and high-resolution metabolomics and lipidomics.

Applications: Discovery and quantitative proteomics, PTM analysis, untargeted metabolomics, lipidomics

View capabilities Discuss a project

FT-ICR Mass Spectrometer

Ultra-high-resolution mass spectrometry

Fourier-transform ion cyclotron resonance mass spectrometer delivering ultra-high mass resolution and mass accuracy for complex mixtures and intact molecules.

Applications: Intact protein and molecular characterization, top-down proteomics, elemental composition, complex-mixture analysis

View capabilities Discuss a project

NMR Spectrometer

Nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy

High-field NMR for non-destructive structural elucidation, metabolite profiling and quantitative analysis of small molecules and biomolecules.

Applications: Structure determination, NMR metabolomics, purity and quantification, compound verification

Genomics Platforms

Next-generation sequencing and library preparation

Sequencing instruments and automated library-preparation workflows for DNA/RNA sequencing, gene-expression and single-cell applications.

Applications: Whole-genome, exome and targeted sequencing, RNA-seq, single-cell RNA-seq, NGS library preparation
