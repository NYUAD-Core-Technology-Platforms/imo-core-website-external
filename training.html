What our training covers

Fundamentals

Introductory courses in mass spectrometry, proteomics, metabolomics, NMR and genomics for researchers new to a technique.

Hands-on instrument training

Practical sessions on sample preparation, instrument operation and data acquisition, leading to independent use where applicable.

Data analysis

Processing, statistics and visualization for proteomics, metabolomics and genomics data, using the software and pipelines the core supports.

Study design

Experimental design for omics studies and scientific data visualization, to plan robust experiments and communicate results.
