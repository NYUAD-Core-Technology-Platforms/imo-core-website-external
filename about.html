About the IMO Core

An integrated multi-omics core facility providing end-to-end scientific support, from experimental design and sample preparation through data acquisition, analysis and interpretation.

Our mission

The Integrated Multi-Omics (IMO) Core provides advanced technologies, scientific expertise, training and bioinformatics support for genomics, proteomics, metabolomics and molecular characterization. We enable researchers to generate high-quality multi-omics data and turn it into biological insight.

The core provides not simply instruments but end-to-end scientific support: we work with you from the first conversation about your research question through experimental design, sample preparation, data acquisition, analysis and interpretation.

Scientific objectives

  • Deliver high-quality, reproducible proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics and genomics data.
  • Support experimental design so that every study is statistically sound and fit for purpose.
  • Integrate data across omics layers to build a systems-level understanding of biology and disease.
  • Develop and share robust methods, workflows and analysis pipelines.
  • Train the next generation of researchers in mass spectrometry, NMR, sequencing and bioinformatics.
  • Act as a scientific collaborator for precision-medicine research across the university and beyond.

Core leadership and team

The IMO Core is staffed by scientists with expertise in mass spectrometry, NMR spectroscopy, genomics and bioinformatics. Team profiles will be published here; in the meantime, please contact us to be put in touch with the right specialist.

User community

The core serves faculty, postdoctoral researchers, students and research staff at NYU Abu Dhabi, as well as collaborators from other institutions, hospitals and industry partners. Projects range from single-platform analyses to multi-omics studies designed with the core from the outset.

Collaborative research

Beyond fee-for-service work, the core partners with research groups on method development, grant applications and long-term studies. If your project would benefit from a scientific collaboration rather than a standard service, tell us about it through the project consultation form.
