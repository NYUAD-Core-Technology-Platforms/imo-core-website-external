Technology areas

Mass spectrometry

Triple-quadrupole / linear ion-trap, trapped-ion-mobility TOF and FT-ICR mass spectrometers coupled to liquid chromatography for proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics and molecular characterization.

NMR spectroscopy

Nuclear magnetic resonance for structural elucidation, metabolite profiling and quantitative analysis of small molecules and biomolecules.

Genomics

Next-generation sequencing platforms and library-preparation workflows for DNA/RNA sequencing, gene-expression and single-cell studies.

Bioinformatics

Computational pipelines, statistics, multi-omics integration and visualization to turn raw data into interpretable results.
